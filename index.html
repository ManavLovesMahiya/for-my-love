Chapter 4
Us
in your arms, the whole world softens 🏡
holding you close to me 🫂

I don’t think I ever really planned on falling for someone the way I’ve fallen for you, Mahiya. It started with just getting to know you, spending time together, and understanding your little habits and the effortless way you are. Somewhere along the way, you quietly became someone who means so much more to me than I ever expected. I love how calm and chill you are, the way you carry yourself, your vibe, and your cute little reactions to things you probably don’t even think twice about. I love seeing you happy, I love making you laugh, and I love knowing that with me, you can just let your guard down and feel completely comfortable being yourself. You’ve become this person I genuinely look forward to talking to every day, seeing, annoying, making memories with, and just having around. And yeah, sometimes I might not say it perfectly or show it in the best way, Mahiya, but I hope you always know that I care about you way more deeply than I could ever put into words.

What I love most about us, Mahiya, is that our love isn’t just about the big romantic moments. I love the random conversations, our stupid jokes, the little nicknames, the teasing, and the moments where we do absolutely nothing special but still somehow have the best time. I love knowing the softer side of you, the sentimental side, the things you love, the way you adore your little soft toys, and all those tiny details that make you uniquely you. You’ve become such a special, irreplaceable part of my life, and I genuinely want to keep discovering more of you as time goes on. I don’t just want the exciting moments with you; I want the ordinary ones too—the random days, the quiet mornings, the long conversations, the silly arguments we eventually end up laughing about, and all the countless memories we haven’t even made yet.

Nothing in this entire world compares to walking into the room and hugging you tight for five minutes straight in complete silence. In that hug, every single worry just melts away. You’ve seen me tired, stressed, and completely vulnerable, and yet with you, I’ve found a peace I never knew existed. I love you for who you are, Mahiya. I love the way you make me feel, and more than anything, I love the fact that out of all the people in this world, somehow I got to find you, hold you, and call you mine. You are my backbone, my peace, and my safe place, baby. Forever and always, just you and me.
